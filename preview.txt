🌱 Horticulture Concept: Polygenic

Pertaining to a phenotypic trait whose inheritance and expression are controlled by the cumulative, additive action of multiple independent genes at different loci (quantitative trait loci or QTLs), each contributing a small fractional effect.

🔬 Structural & Conceptual Basis:
Unlike monogenic (qualitative) traits—which follow simple Mendelian inheritance and exhibit discrete, discontinuous phenotypic classes (such as yellow vs. green cotyledons, or single-gene vertical disease resistance)—polygenic traits display continuous variation across a population, forming a classic bell-shaped Gaussian distribution curve.

⚙️ Physiological Mechanism:
Physiologically and statistically, the phenotypic value (P) of a polygenic trait is the cumulative product of the genotypic value (G), the environmental variance (E), and the genotype-by-environment interaction (G × E).

🌾 Field & Crop Context:
In Bangladeshi horticultural breeding programs, mastering polygenic inheritance is essential for developing climate-resilient cultivars.
